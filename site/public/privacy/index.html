What Appeal Draft records

The website. Page views go to Vercel Web Analytics and to PostHog: the page, the step you reached (home found, checkout opened, paid), the link you arrived from, your device type. Not the address you type. Payments go through Stripe, which keeps your email for the receipt; we keep the email only to send your password.

The AI connector (https://appeal-draft.vercel.app/mcp). Each call records which tool ran, whether it worked, how long it took, the county and the verdict (for example "argue" or "backs"), and which assistant called (Claude, ChatGPT). The address you ask about is used to find the home and is not stored. When no ZIP is given, the address is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder to find one. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

The data. Public record from the county assessors and the recorded sales. Owner names are never shown or returned.
