How the numbers are made

The county's value is the estimated market value on the assessor's roll (). "Homes like yours" are recorded arm's-length sales since of homes within 25% of your finished square feet and 25 years of your build year, within 2 miles, nearest and most alike first; the green number is their median price per square foot times your square feet. Sales under $50,000, or far off the sold home's own value (outside 0.5 to 2 times), are left out as family transfers or partial interests. Your ZIP's number is the median $/sq ft of every such sale in the ZIP. Prices are not adjusted for time. Condos are not covered.
